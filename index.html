LACeS Anycast Census

Opt-out information for network operators

What is this traffic?

You are seeing probe packets originating from the LACeS (Longitudinal Anycast Census System) project. LACeS performs a daily census of IPv4 and IPv6 anycast deployments across the Internet. The goal is to provide an open, publicly available dataset of anycast prefixes for network operators and the research community.

What do we send?

Our probes consist of:

  • ICMP echo requests (ping) — one per /24 (IPv4) or /48 (IPv6) prefix.
  • TCP SYN/ACK packets — to a single address per prefix on a non-standard high port.
  • DNS queries — to known public DNS nameservers only.

Each target address receives at most a few packets per day. The traffic volume is minimal and should not impact your network operations.

Why are we doing this?

LACeS is a research project run by the Design and Analysis of Communication Systems (DACS) group at the University of Twente, the Netherlands. The census data helps operators understand their own anycast deployments and supports Internet measurement research. Results are published at manycast.net and the underlying dataset is freely available.

The methodology is described in:
Hendriks, Luckie, Jonker, Sommese, van Rijswijk-Deij. LACeS: An Open, Fast, Responsible and Efficient Longitudinal Anycast Census System. ACM Internet Measurement Conference (IMC), 2025.

Measurement infrastructure

  • TANGLED Anycast Testbed — 32 globally distributed nodes used for anycast-based (AB) detection.
  • CAIDA Archipelago (Ark) — vantage points used for latency-based (GCD) geolocation.

How to opt out

If you would like your prefix(es) excluded from future LACeS probing, please send an email to the address below with the prefix(es) you want excluded. We will add them to our exclusion list and they will be removed from subsequent census runs.
